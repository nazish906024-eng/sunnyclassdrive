Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

SunnyClass provides a shared school-file library. This policy explains what information the site handles and how files are made available. Please read it before signing in or uploading files.

1. Information handled

  • Account information: If you sign in, Firebase Authentication processes the sign-in details for the method you choose, such as email, Google, or phone authentication.
  • File information: When a signed-in user uploads a file, the service processes the file and related library details, such as its name, type, size, uploader reference, and upload time.
  • Device storage: SunnyClass stores your favourites list in your browser's local storage on that device. The site also uses a service worker to cache website files so the app can load more reliably.

2. Public files and sharing

The library is designed for public browsing and downloads. Files uploaded to the public school library, and their associated library information, may be visible to anyone who visits the site. Do not upload private, confidential, personal, or sensitive information, including student records, addresses, phone numbers, or documents, unless you have permission and the school has approved sharing them publicly.

3. Service providers

SunnyClass uses third-party services to operate the site: GitHub Pages for website hosting, Firebase Authentication and Firestore for sign-in and library records, and Supabase Storage and its upload function for file storage. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy terms and policies.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses browser local storage for favourites and a service worker cache for website resources. Authentication providers may use cookies or similar technologies as part of sign-in and security. The current app code does not intentionally send analytics events.

5. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate uploaders, store and display shared files, show file details, remember favourites on your device, and keep the website functioning. This policy does not promise that public uploads are private.

6. Retention and deletion

Uploaded files and library records may remain available until they are removed by an authorized site or service administrator. Favourites can be cleared through your browser's site-data settings. Account information is handled by the authentication provider. For questions about removal of an uploaded file or account data, contact the person or organization that operates SunnyClass through a channel they have provided.

7. Children's privacy and safe use

SunnyClass may be used in school settings. Students should upload only materials approved by their teacher or school and should not include personal or sensitive information. A parent, guardian, or school administrator should help determine what information may be shared publicly.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or service providers change. The latest version and its update date will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

If you need help with privacy questions or removal of a file, please use an official contact channel provided by the school or the SunnyClass site operator. No direct contact form is provided on this page.
